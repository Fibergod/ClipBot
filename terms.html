Terms of Service

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Clip Bot is a private tool operated by its owner for their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

  • Use of the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • The app only clips streamers whose official clipping campaigns the owner has joined, follows each campaign's rules, credits every streamer and links the original stream, and labels paid campaign clips as paid content.
  • Any rights holder who believes a clip should be removed can contact https://github.com/Fibergod/ClipBot/issues (open an issue) and it will be taken down.
